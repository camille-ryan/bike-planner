Scenicness signals

Per-edge rasters produced by scenicness-bake. Each layer is a single PNG image overlay covering the bbox (currently the Graz↔Wien corridor). Toggle on demand — PNGs are tens of MB. Families:

  • Forest (green): forest_local 200 m disc, forest_wide 2 km disc.
  • Terrain: local_relief 500 m σ, regional_relief 10 km σ, distance_to_drama meters to nearest cell with local_relief ≥ 80 m, view_dominance = DEM − 2 km Gaussian (ridge prominence; ±).
  • Water (blue): water_local / water_wide = lakes & wide-river polygons. sea_local / sea_wide = ocean/sea (separated so the cost function can weight ocean vistas above lake vistas). waterway_local = 200 m disc of buffered river/canal/stream lines. waterway_along_edge = fraction of this edge that literally runs inside a 10 m-buffered waterway polygon — the "Mur cycle path" detector.
  • Wetland (teal): wetland_local = 200 m disc of natural=wetland polygons (marsh, reedbed, bog, saltmarsh, …).
